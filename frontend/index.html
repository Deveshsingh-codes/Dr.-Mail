Model Insights

TF-IDF

Turns text into numbers: words (and word pairs) weighted by how often they appear in a message versus across all messages.

Naive Bayes

Learns how likely each term is in spam vs. ham, then combines those likelihoods with Bayes' rule, assuming terms are independent.

Predicting

Your email is vectorized with the saved vectorizer, and the model returns estimated class probabilities.
